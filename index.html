easyTransfer

Send files over Wi-Fi. No cables, no cloud, no account.

How it works

  1. Receive — open the app on one device and tap Receive; it shows a QR code.
  2. Send — on the other device, tap Send and scan that code.
  3. Accept & go — the receiver taps Accept, the sender picks files, and they land straight in Downloads.

easyTransfer EC

Send files over the internet. Mobile data, different networks — anywhere.

How it works

  1. Receive — open the app on one device and tap Receive; it shows a QR code.
  2. Send — on the other device, anywhere with a connection, tap Send and scan that code.
  3. Accept & go — the receiver taps Accept, the sender picks files, and they transfer end-to-end encrypted, direct when possible.

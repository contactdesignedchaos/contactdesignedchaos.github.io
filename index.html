easyTransfer

Send files over Wi-Fi or the internet. No cables, no cloud storage, no account.

How it works

  1. Receive — open the app on one device and tap Receive; it shows a QR code.
  2. Send — on the other device, tap Send and scan that code. Same Wi-Fi or not, it connects automatically.
  3. Accept & go — the receiver taps Accept, the sender picks files, and they land straight in Downloads.
